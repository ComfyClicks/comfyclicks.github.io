Operational impact

Built to make better buying decisions.

I built this buying tool after finding that I had no reliable way to track orders or connect purchasing decisions with sales and spoilage. I expanded it over time as new operational needs surfaced.

The Difference column became the key decision-making feature, making overbuying and underbuying visible at a glance. It enabled just-in-time ordering, with fresh product moving directly from truck to shelf instead of sitting in back stock and requiring a second pack-out.

The resulting improvement in product freshness helped increase sales, while more precise ordering reduced spoilage. Together, these improvements made the operation more efficient and increased profitability. The Lost Margin analysis also helped identify products whose sales and spoilage patterns were eroding profitability.
